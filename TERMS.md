## 1. About OrcaBox & Platform Nature

OrcaBox serves as your premier open-source hub for cinematic information, personal library tracking, and a comprehensive guide to legal streaming and media discovery. For both the casual moviegoer and the passionate aficionado of anime, movies, television series, manga, and light novels, our platform is crafted to help you discover, investigate, organize, and track media spanning every genre imaginable—from action and drama to comedy, sci-fi, and fantasy. OrcaBox is your unified source for rich media data, expert information, synopsis, ratings, and streaming availability guidance.

Our application is built with a sleek, intuitive design that lets you effortlessly browse titles by genre, release date, community ratings, or even by your favorite actors, directors, and studios across phone, tablet, and TV screens. When you need information on a specific title, OrcaBox delivers everything you need: cast lists, plot summaries, critical reviews, viewer ratings, and guidance on where to legally stream it across official licensed platforms (such as Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, Crunchyroll, Hulu, and many others).

## 2. A Community Built on Passion

At OrcaBox, we cultivate a vibrant community where movie and media lovers can share insightful reviews and thoughtful recommendations. Our open-source ecosystem connects cinephiles and fans from all corners of the globe, sparking rich conversations about storytelling, animation, filmmaking, and the magic of cinema. We uphold high standards to ensure quality content and a respectful environment for everyone.

## 3. Your Privacy and Security Matter

We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and personal data:

- **Local-First Storage:** All personal data—including watch history, reading progress, bookmarked titles, and customized preferences—is stored locally on your device in private on-device storage.
- **Zero Profiling:** We do not monitor, profile, harvest, or sell your personal information or viewing behavior to data brokers or advertisers.
- **Fast and Clean:** Our platform offers a clean, secure browsing experience optimized for speed and seamless navigation on any device—from handheld touchscreens to remote-controlled Android TVs.

## 4. Go Beyond the Screen

OrcaBox is more than just a tracking database. We bring you:
- Synchronized watch progress and personal library management across devices.
- Calendars for upcoming releases, season debuts, and episode airings.
- Multi-mode flexibility with dedicated layouts for **Streaming**, **Manga**, and **Novels**.
- Seamless remote navigation specifically engineered for 10-foot television interfaces.
